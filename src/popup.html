Each ticked video is saved to its own file, straight from the stream Meet delivers. Tick or untick while recording to add or finish a file. A control bar on the meeting page does the same without opening this popup.

Sites GMRec can record

Google Meet and ADPList work out of the box. For anything else, paste the meeting page's address — Chrome will ask permission for that site, and GMRec reads the participant tiles the same way. Names depend on what the site renders; where it exposes none, tiles are listed as Participant 1, 2, …

Recordings go inside your browser's Downloads folder — an extension cannot write anywhere else. Use a path like Work/Meetings to file them, turn the switch on to pick a destination for every file, or change the download location in Chrome settings to move all of them at once.

Participants and screen shares are always saved at the quality the meeting delivers, so this affects only your own camera file and the encoding bitrate. 720p is smoothest on most laptops.
